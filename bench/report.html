No judge, no taste: a deterministic matcher credits each finding to at most one seed. Nobody scores full marks, and that is the point. What matters is the distance to the claude baseline, and what the points cost. The headline score is every target, because the go+design pair it used to be misranked six of these models by three places or more once the other six languages disagreed with it — the per-language matrix below is where a model that is strong in one language and weak in another becomes visible.

Per point is what one found defect cost. $ is money actually billed to a card, which is OpenRouter and only OpenRouter. ~$ is notional: the route bills a plan rather than the tokens, so the figure is what the same run would have cost at published rates — the only way to compare a seat paid by plan against one paid in credits. That covers Claude, ChatGPT and ollama: ollama published per-token rates on 2026-09-01, but it still meters a session quota and a weekly quota, and its paid “Extra usage” is a separate balance that starts empty. A sweep there spends quota, not money. Every route in this table is priced in the same unit; only one of them is an invoice.

Contract failure is the disqualifier, independent of score: a session that returns unparseable output burns a full slot and can flip a panel verdict to inconclusive. Both models that failed here have failed before.

Wall clock matters as much as tokens: a panel round runs at the pace of its slowest reviewer.

! marks a cell whose run lost a lens to a contract failure. That recall is deflated, not low — the findings the failed lens would have contributed were never parsed — so it is not comparable to a clean cell and is excluded from the field median. Re-run the target to get a real number; the failure itself still counts against the model.
